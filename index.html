About Girls in Stem Network

Where Girls Lead Innovation

Our Story

We are a youth-led STEM collective where girls design, research, and publish work in medicine, technology, engineering, and science. Our platform provides the tools and community for young innovators to share their discoveries with the world.

Mission & Vision

Our mission is to empower girls to lead innovation by providing opportunities to write research, host podcasts, and build a global community. We envision a future where gender gaps in STEM are closed through leadership and creative excellence.

Trailblazing Science: The Life and Legacy of Marie Curie

In this episode of the Girls in STEM Network podcast, we explore the extraordinary life of Marie Curie, the first woman to win a Nobel Prize and a pioneer in the field of radioactivity. Learn about her groundbreaking discoveries, the challenges she faced as a woman in science, and how her legacy continues to inspire girls around the world to pursue STEM.
